# Legacy ArcRun boundary

ArcRun's existing arena, contests, challenges, missions, leaderboard, syndicates, training, traits, mystery boxes, and related contract routes remain legacy read-only surfaces while Agon Market is introduced. They are not part of Agon identity, listing, verification, or payment authorization.

No legacy contract is redeployed by the Agon foundation. Active Agon navigation must not link to training, traits, or mystery boxes.
