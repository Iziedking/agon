# AGON landing page: motion and copy brief

**User and action.** A first-time visitor should understand that AGON helps
them find an agent service, inspect its terms, and test it before deciding to
pay. The first action is opening the market through Find a service; the market
owns task search. Browsing stays open without an account.

**Meaning.** Separate agent services become useful when AGON brings their
terms and evidence into one place.

**Market direction.** AGON aims to aggregate services from connected agent
marketplaces. Search should combine source-backed records in one comparison
view, identify the source of each service, and keep AGON listing and test
evidence distinct from claims imported from another market. Until an adapter
is connected and checked, the page must describe that source as planned rather
than imply its services are already searchable.

**Emotional contract.** The page should feel precise and easy to enter. A
visitor should never mistake a decorative animation for a live network,
verified service, or completed payment.

**Focal object.** The supplied 14-second video shows two broad paths making a
square through alternating rounded corners. AGON translates that geometry
into quiet, interlocking route lines in the page background. The visual stands for
discovery converging on one usable service. It is drawn with AGON's existing
pink and neutral palette, not the video's purple and cyan field.

**Hierarchy.** Preserve the existing AGON mark, display type, squared borders,
pink primary action, and desktop split. The hero states one promise, then the
market handles search, pricing, testing, and delivery details. Keep the MCP guide
available without implying that AGON already provisions a publisher wallet.
AGON uses its dark palette throughout the product for now. Do not show a theme
switch, and do not apply a previously saved light preference on AGON pages.
The hero's right side gives the route motif room to breathe. Network context
stays in the header, and the MCP path stays in its own guide and provider
section. Do not repeat either as prose in the hero.

**Motion.** The main paths sit inside the hero's right panel, clear of its
vertical divider. Fainter, offset copies recur behind the hiring steps and
provider close so the motion belongs to the whole page without covering text.
Short pink segments move slowly along each path. Pointer movement and glows
are removed. The animation stays behind content, does not change layout, and stops under
`prefers-reduced-motion`. There is no forced intro or wait before opening the market.

**Trust and failure.** Do not call an untested service verified. The market
link remains usable if motion fails. The page must say that buyers review terms before paid
use; payment and delivery remain separate records. Publisher copy must not
claim wallet provisioning or automatic onchain publication.

**Responsive and access.** At narrow widths the existing split becomes a
reading sequence, with the primary market action visible and 44px targets.
The help icon sits in the footer, where it cannot cover the main action.
The route graphic is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Text and
focus states must stand without the animation.

**Rejected directions.** A full-bleed copy of the video would replace AGON's
identity. A 3-second splash delays the first useful action. Ambient particles,
large glows, and synthetic activity signals make the page busier without
explaining the service.
